🎄 Christmas Launch

The Show Gets Synced

  • Over 6,000 blue and white icicles
  • Total of 9,235 LED lights

Pixels, Controllers & Cables

  • Falcon F16V3, DMX controllers, and another Light-O-Rama AC controller

  • 800 pixels for pillar matrices
  • 8 new leaping arches for the middle of the yard
