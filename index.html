About The Show

Hidden Lake Lights is a free Halloween and Christmas light display open to the public. Visitors can park, tune their radio to 90.1 FM, and watch thousands of lights flash in sync with music.

Location: 28 Lost Valley Ct, Saint Peters, MO 63376
