About The Show

Holiday lights with serious sparkle.

Hidden Lake Lights is a free Halloween and Christmas light display open to the public. Visitors can park, tune their radio to 90.1 FM, and enjoy a synchronized show from the comfort of their car! The show was created by Jacob Jones in 2018 when he was just 15 years old! Jacob loved getting to go look at other light shows in the area with his family, and eventually gathered up the funds to create his own to support a good cause!
