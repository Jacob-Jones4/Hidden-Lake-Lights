HIDDEN LAKE LIGHTS

HALLOWEEN AND CHRISTMAS LIGHT DISPLAY IN ST PETERS MO

About Us

Hidden Lake Lights is a free Holiday Light display open to the public! Just sit in the warmth of your car and watch over 17,000 lights flash in sync with music! Be sure to tune your radio to 90.1 FM to hear the music.

Donate!

As a way to give back to our community, we collect donations for the Sts. Joachim and Ann Care Service! We have a donation box and QR code located in front of the display to drop off cash, checks, canned food, and toys for families in need during the Holiday Season. You can donate online HERE!
