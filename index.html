About The Show

Holiday lights with serious sparkle.

Hidden Lake Lights is a free Halloween and Christmas light display open to the public. Visitors can park, tune their radio to 90.1 FM, and enjoy a synchronized show from the comfort of their car.

📍

Location

28 Lost Valley Ct, Saint Peters, MO 63376

🎵

Audio

Tune to 90.1 FM when you arrive.

🚗

Viewing

Enjoy the show from your vehicle and please be respectful of our neighbors!
